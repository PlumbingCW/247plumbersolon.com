Pick the spot before you pick the heater

The right heater is the one that fits the room it lives in. Check the clearance around it for service, the strength of the floor beneath, and how far the hot lines run to the kitchen and baths. A long run wastes water while you wait for heat.

In a finished basement or remodel, moving the unit often means a new vent path or a new drain route. Note where your shutoff valves and floor drain sit and bring photos to the call. That can save a second trip.

Gas, electric, or heat pump

Fuel choice shapes everything from venting to the monthly bill. Each type has a place, and the best pick depends on what your house already has.

Gas tank or tankless

Gas heats quickly and recovers fast, but it needs a flue and a correctly sized gas line. A tankless gas unit often needs a larger line than the tank it replaces.

Electric tank

An electric heater needs no flue, which makes placement easier. It does need panel capacity and a circuit that suits the elements.

Heat pump tank

A heat pump model moves heat from the room air into the water. It needs open space around it and a condensate drain, and it cools the room a little as it works.

Local note

Permits and registration in Solon

Solon's Required Residential Permits Guide lists all new plumbing installations and water heater replacement as work needing a plumbing permit. A new heater, a moved heater, or a swapped one all fall under that kind of work. The city Building Department can confirm which form applies to your project.

Contractors working in Solon register with the city every year and show a current Ohio plumbing license. Ask to see both before you sign, and ask who files the permit.
