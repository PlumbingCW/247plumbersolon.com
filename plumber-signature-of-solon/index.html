The community

What Signature of Solon is, and what we could confirm

The Signature of Solon Homeowners' Association runs the community's gates and publishes gate instructions for residents and service people. Real estate listings describe it as a golf course community with a clubhouse, pool and tennis courts, and the homes shown in those listings are large. One recent listing for a Signature II home built in 2002 shows over 7,000 square feet, six bathrooms and a finished lower level.

We could not find a published street map, a home count or a documented age range for the whole community, so we do not guess at them. Listings group homes under Signature of Solon and Signature of Solon II, with entrances on Route 43 and at Pettibone Road and Stockwood Drive. If your home is in either section, this page applies to you.

Gate access

Before a plumber arrives, sort out the gate

The association's own gate page says service people should use the main entrance on Route 43 or the Signature II entrance at Pettibone and Stockwood. The other gates are for residents only. A few minutes of preparation keeps a leak call from stalling at the entrance.

  • ✓Tell us your street when you call. We will confirm which entrance to use so the truck does not sit at a resident-only gate.
  • ✓Have your access ready. The association says residents use stickers or personal codes. Be ready to enter your code or approve the visitor from your phone or app.
  • ✓Renting the home? The association says tenants need the owner to apply for gate access on their behalf, so check this before an emergency, not during one.
  • ✓Gate trouble. The gate page lists the management company for gate problems. Keep that number with your plumbing numbers.

None of this changes what you do first in an emergency. Close the main valve, keep clear of wet outlets, and call (440) 468-0237.

Larger homes

Questions that come with bigger houses

These are general plumbing points for large homes. They are not claims about failures in this community.

Know every shutoff

A home with many bathrooms has many fixture stops as well as the main. Label them so a leak under one vanity does not mean shutting off the whole house.

A finished lower level raises the stakes

Water that reaches finished space costs more to dry out. Find your floor drain and any sump pit now, and test the pump each spring. See sump pump repair if it stays silent.

Hard water works on every fixture

Cleveland Water puts its hardness near 7 grains per gallon. More fixtures means more aerators and valves that can scale up, and a heater that works harder.

Sprinklers and backflow devices

Solon's permit guide lists irrigation systems with a backflow device. Ohio's rule calls for testing backflow preventers at least once every twelve months, so ask when yours was last tested.

Local note

Digging for a sewer repair

If a lateral needs to be dug up, OHIO811 asks you to call 811 at least two working days before digging, and that includes hand digging. The call is free. A contractor will usually submit it, but ask who is doing it, since utility lines under a wooded lot can sit where you do not expect.

Inside a community with an association, ask whether the board needs notice of exterior work before a crew arrives.
