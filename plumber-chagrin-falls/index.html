Storm history

What the July 2023 cloudburst means for your sump pump

On July 25, 2023, more than two inches of rain and hail fell in about 30 minutes. Water flooded businesses on South Main Street and Bell Street, and the mayor's own basement. The mayor pointed to a 1994 dam burst as the last event that compared.

A sump pump only helps if it has power, a free-moving float, and a discharge line that goes somewhere. Pour a bucket of water into the pit this week and watch it run. If it stalls, our sump pump repair crew can replace the pump before the next storm, not during it.

Two water sources, one tap

The Village of Chagrin Falls Water Company blends Cleveland Water, drawn from Lake Erie, with groundwater from its own Franklin Street well field. The two are mixed at the Solon Road Booster Station. The system runs about 34 miles of line and serves about 2,500 customers.

The village report does not list a hardness figure for the blend. Cleveland Water's own supply is about 7 grains per gallon, and groundwater is often harder, so your tap may differ from Solon's. Test before you buy a softener, and ask the village utilities if taste changes.

The village reports no lead service lines that need replacement, and none of 26 sites sampled in 2024 went over the action level. That covers the village side. Pipe inside an 1800s house is a separate question, and we can tell you what is there.

Local note

The village plumbing permit and what it asks of you

Chagrin Falls issues a residential plumbing permit, and plumbing contractors must register with the village before starting. Inspections need 24 hours' notice, and processing can take up to 10 business days. The Building and Zoning office answers weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Plan for that delay on any remodel. A leak or a failed fixture is a repair and can go the same day, but ask the village whether your job needs a permit before we start. Our plumbing installation team can walk you through the sequence.

A village sewer system that is fighting extra water

The village owns its sewers and wastewater plant, not a regional district. The plant was built in 1972 and updated in 1992, and the 2022 annual report says untreated sewage can reach the Chagrin River in extreme overflows. Relining a section of East Washington Street kept about 400,000 gallons of groundwater out of the sewer.

That matters in your house because clean water should not enter a sanitary line. Keep sump discharge and downspouts pointed outside, and call us if you are unsure where yours goes. If a backup hits, the drain cleaning visit starts at your cleanout.

Before we arrive

If water is coming in before the plumber reaches you

1Cut the power to wet areas

If the water is near the panel or outlets, keep out. Switch off only what you can reach while dry.

2Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise if a pipe is the source. If rain is the source, skip this and keep the pump running.

3Check the pump and its outlet

Make sure it is plugged in and the discharge hose is not kinked or frozen. Do not reach in while the pump is live.

4Photograph and lift

Photos first for the insurer, then raise boxes, rugs, and electronics off the floor.
