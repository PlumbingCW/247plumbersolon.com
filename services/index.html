How these pages are organized

Urgent repairs come first in the list because water damage grows by the hour. Planned work such as a new water heater, a repipe or a sump pump upgrade sits further down, where you can compare options without a deadline.

If you are not sure which page fits, start with the symptom. A gurgling drain points to drain cleaning. A water bill that jumped with no change in habits points to leak detection. No hot water points to water heater repair.

Emergency and planned work, side by side

Emergency work means active water, sewage or a failed heater. Planned work means you chose the date. We handle both from the same office on Aurora Road, and the plumber who answers the phone can tell you which category your problem is in.

Homes across Solon, Twinsburg and Aurora range from older mid-century builds to newer subdivisions, and the pipes behind the walls differ just as much. That is why we diagnose first and quote after, instead of quoting from a phone description alone.
