Where it sits

The Preserve of Solon in plain terms

A community profile places The Preserve of Solon just south of Aurora Road, which is Route 43, in central Solon, with two entrances off Liberty Road and one off Pettibone Road. Another listing site describes walking trails to the park, library and schools. We could not find a city document that sets the boundary, so these anchors are the ones to trust.

The profiles describe single-family colonial and two-story homes. We do not know your pipe material, and we will not guess it. A look under the sink and at the basement wall tells more than the build year does.

Age, not alarm

What a home from the 1990s or early 2000s tends to need

These are general points about homes of this age. They are not a count of problems in this subdivision.

The original water heater

A tank in a home built in 1992 to 2003 may well have been replaced already. If yours is the original, it is far past the age most tanks last, and a leak at the base is the sign to plan a water heater replacement.

Scale from moderately hard water

Cleveland Water puts its hardness near 7 grains per gallon. Over a few decades that builds scale on heating elements and inside fixtures, so a flush or new aerators often help.

Faucets, toilets and supply stops

Rubber and plastic parts wear on a different schedule from the pipes. A faucet repair or toilet repair is often a part, not a rebuild.

Slow drains that keep coming back

Repeated clogs in a main drain can mean grease, roots or a pipe that has shifted. A camera look through our sewer inspection service shows which.

How a water heater swap works in Solon

Solon's Required Residential Permits Guide lists replacing a water heater under plumbing, so a swap is not only a plumber's job.

  1. 1

    Confirm the tank is the problem

    A drip from the bottom is the shell. A drip from a fitting may be a part. We check before we recommend anything.

  2. 2

    Agree the price

    You hear the price before work starts, including whether a permit is part of it.

  3. 3

    Permit and install

    The permit goes through the Solon Building Department. Contractors must register with the city each year and show a current Ohio license.

  4. 4

    Inspection

    Call in the inspection listed on your permit. Do not skip it.

Local note

Sewer backup: the city line comes first

The City of Solon says the homeowner is responsible for the lateral from the house to the clean out. If sewage backs into your basement, the city asks you to call its Service Department, whose crews check the city main first.

If the main is clear, the stoppage is on your side and we can clear it. See drain cleaning and hydro jetting for what that looks like.

Before we arrive

Do these three things first

1Close the main water valve

It is usually where the line enters the basement wall. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2Stay away from wet electrics

If water reaches outlets or the panel, leave the area and call the electric utility.

3Stop using drains

In a backup, every flush and every load of laundry adds to it.
