How to reach us

For anything involving active water, sewage or a dead heater, call. A plumber answers and tells you what to do while we get to you. There are no forms on this site on purpose, because a form cannot tell you to close your main valve.

For questions that are not urgent, such as service areas or planned work, email contact@247plumbersolon.com and we will reply as soon as the office can.

What to have ready when you call

Know your street address and the nearest cross street. Be ready to say where the water is coming from and whether you found the main shutoff. If a smell of gas is involved, leave the building first and call 911 or the gas utility from outside.
