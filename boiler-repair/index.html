Safety first

No heat tonight: what to check and what to avoid

Some boiler faults are simple, and some are not. Start with safety, then try the easy checks.

  • ✓Smell gas? Leave.Walk out of the house without touching switches, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • ✓Check your carbon monoxide alarm.If it sounds, get everyone outside and call 911. Do not try to restart the boiler.
  • ✓Look at the thermostat.Confirm it is set to heat, the batteries are fresh and the setting is above room temperature.
  • ✓Read the pressure gauge.Most home boilers sit near the mid-range when cold. A reading near zero or in the red zone tells us what to bring.
  • ✓Do not bleed or drain it yourself.Letting water out changes the pressure and can leave the boiler running dry.

Symptoms that point to a boiler fault

Cold radiators or baseboard

Air in the system, a failed circulator or a closed zone valve can stop hot water from reaching the rooms.

Banging or kettle-like noises

Mineral buildup or trapped air makes the heat exchanger noisy and signals it is working too hard.

Puddle under the boiler

A weeping valve, a failing tank or a leaking fitting. Leaks rarely fix themselves.

Pressure gauge dropping again

A slow leak, a worn expansion tank or a relief valve that is passing water.

Boiler cycles on and off

Short cycling can come from an oversized setting, a failing sensor or poor water flow.

Why a boiler fails in the middle of winter

Boilers run hardest when it is coldest, so weak parts get found out in the first deep freeze. A hot water boiler pushes heated water through pipes to radiators or baseboards, and it relies on a pump, valves, a pressure-regulated loop and a safe burner. Any one of those can fail without warning.

Cleveland Hopkins airport has a January mean low of 22.3 degrees Fahrenheit per the National Weather Service. A house that loses heat while pipes run through outside walls can freeze fast, so do not wait a day to see if the boiler starts again.

Local note

Permits and licenses to ask about in Solon

Solon requires contractors to register with the city each year and to show a current Ohio license before working. Ohio's Construction Industry Licensing Board licenses plumbing and hydronics contractors, and you can look up any contractor on the state's eLicense center before you hire. Doing that takes two minutes and tells you whether the person at your door is permitted to work.

The city's permit guide lists permits for plumbing installations and for replacing a water heater. It does not say how a boiler replacement is handled, so ask the Solon Building Department before a full replacement is booked.

A boiler is not a water heater

Plenty of people mix the two up. A water heater makes hot water for taps and showers, while a boiler heats water in a closed loop for the house. If you also need hot water repaired, read about water heater repair. If a burst pipe has soaked the boiler room, see burst pipe repair first.
