Sewage is backing up: what to do before the truck arrives

Raw sewage carries bacteria, so the first job is limiting how far it spreads and how many people touch it.

  • ✓Stop all water use.Every flush, shower and washing machine cycle sends more water toward a line that has nowhere to put it.
  • ✓Keep people out of the wet zone.Children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system should stay clear, and wet shoes should stay off the carpet.
  • ✓Turn off power only if the floor is dry where you stand.If water reaches outlets or a furnace, stay out and tell us when you call.
  • ✓Note where it shows first.Sewage at the lowest drain only suggests the main line. Backup in one bathroom only suggests a branch line.

If you smell gas along with the sewage odor, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call anyone else.

How a failing sewer line gives itself away

Most breaks and blockages warn you for weeks. These are the patterns plumbers read first.

Gurgling after a flush

Air pushed back through a toilet or tub drain means the main line is partly blocked downstream.

Several drains slow at once

One slow sink is usually local. Slow sinks, tubs and a toilet together point at the shared line.

Sewage smell in the yard

A damp, sour patch of lawn along the line can mean a cracked or separated pipe underground.

Greener, faster grass in a strip

Leaking sewer water feeds the turf above it, so a lush stripe between house and street is worth a camera look.

Repeat clogs after every cleaning

If a drain clears and clogs again in a few weeks, roots or a belly in the pipe are more likely than hair or grease.

Local note

In Solon, you own the lateral and the city owns the main

Per the City of Solon's own FAQ, the property owner is responsible for the lateral running from the house to the clean out. If sewage is backing up into your basement, the city asks you to call its Service Department. Crews check the city main first, and if it is clear, they recommend a licensed plumber.

That order saves money. A camera run on your lateral wastes a visit if the street main is the problem, and a city crew cannot fix a cracked pipe on your side. We tell you honestly when your symptoms point toward the main instead of your own line.

Why roots and old pipe show up in Solon yards

Solon's median home was built in 1981 per an older Census profile, and about 38 percent of homes date to the 1970s and 1980s. Homes of that era often have laterals in clay, cast iron or early plastic. Those older materials develop joint gaps where roots can enter, so a repeat clog is worth a camera look.

Lots here tend to be large, and many have mature trees. Solon's sewer code even notes that heavy cast iron pipe may be required where sewers cross unstable soil or run close to trees. A big oak near your lateral is a fair reason to ask for footage before accepting another cabling.

Before any digging starts

Dial 811 at least two working days before opening ground. Ohio811 notifies utilities and marks their lines for free, and that applies even to hand digging. Digging into a sewer is also permit work in Solon, so ask the Building Department what applies at your address. See also our sewer line cleaning service for lines that only need clearing.
